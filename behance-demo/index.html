Mobile App Design / UX Case Study

BarberQueue

A mobile booking platform connecting customers with local barber shops in Vietnam

Role UX/UI Designer
Duration 8 Weeks
Scope Mobile App
Tools Figma, FigJam

Project Overview

BarberQueue is a mobile application that digitizes the barbershop booking experience for the Vietnamese market. The platform serves two user groups: customers seeking convenient appointment scheduling, and barbershop owners needing efficient calendar management.

The Challenge

Traditional booking methods (phone calls, walk-ins) create friction for busy customers and operational chaos for barbershops. No localized digital solution exists for the Vietnamese market.

The Solution

A dual-interface mobile app enabling real-time availability viewing, instant booking, automated reminders, and streamlined schedule management for shops.

The Outcome

A functional MVP prototype validated through user testing, demonstrating reduced booking friction and improved shop efficiency through automated notifications.

Problem Statement

Understanding the pain points from both sides of the booking experience

Customer Pain Points
  • Phone calls often go unanswered during busy hours
  • No visibility into barber availability or wait times
  • Long waits during peak hours (evenings, weekends)
  • Forgotten appointments due to lack of reminders
  • Difficulty finding reputable shops with transparent pricing
Barbershop Pain Points
  • Manual scheduling via notebooks or scattered messages
  • High no-show rates causing revenue loss
  • No automated reminder system
  • Difficulty tracking individual barber schedules
  • Limited tools for small/medium shops (1-5 barbers)

Target Users

Defining our primary user segments for the Vietnamese urban market

The Busy Professional

Male/Female, 22-40 years old

Context: Office workers and young professionals in Hanoi/HCMC with limited free time.

Needs: Quick booking without phone calls, visibility into wait times, appointment reminders.

Behavior: Heavy smartphone users, value convenience over price, prefer planning ahead.

The Shop Owner

Small/Medium Barbershop (1-5 barbers)

Context: Independent barbershop owners managing daily operations manually.

Needs: Simple scheduling tool, reduced no-shows, customer tracking.

Behavior: Limited tech budget, uses smartphone for business, wants low-friction adoption.

Design Principles

Clarity First

Every screen answers one question. Information hierarchy guides users through booking without confusion.

Speed Over Polish

Minimal taps to complete core actions. Visual flourishes never slow down the experience.

Trust Through Transparency

Prices, availability, and ratings visible upfront. No hidden information or surprise fees.

Outcome & Reflection

What Was Achieved

  • Completed MVP design covering both customer and shop owner interfaces
  • Validated core booking flow through usability testing (8 participants, 85% task completion)
  • Established scalable design system for future feature expansion
  • Created comprehensive Figma prototype demonstrating full user journey

Key Learnings

  • Scope discipline matters: Focusing on core booking prevented feature creep in an 8-week timeline
  • Two-sided platforms are complex: Balancing customer simplicity with shop flexibility required careful trade-offs
  • Local context is critical: Vietnamese market assumptions (cash preference, walk-in culture) shaped key design decisions

Future Opportunities

  • Payment integration (MoMo, bank transfer) for prepaid bookings
  • Review and rating system with photo uploads
  • Loyalty program and promotional features
  • Analytics dashboard for shop owners

Constraints & Limitations

As a student project, this case study has inherent limitations:

  • User research sample size was limited (20 participants)
  • No production implementation or real-world metrics
  • Payment and notification systems designed but not functional
  • Market validation limited to Hanoi/HCMC urban areas
