The Problem We're Solving

Traditional barber booking is broken for everyone involved

👤

For Customers

  • Phone calls that go unanswered
  • Long waits at peak hours
  • No visibility into barber availability
  • Forgotten appointments without reminders
✂

For Barber Shops

  • Manual scheduling via notebooks or messages
  • High no-show rates causing revenue loss
  • No automated reminder system
  • Difficulty tracking barber schedules

One App. Two Solutions.

BarberQueue digitizes the entire booking experience for both customers and shops
